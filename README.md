## About me

I am a software engineer with more than 10 years of experience in technology and a background as a Tech Lead. I mainly work with APIs, system architecture, cloud, and automation — always looking to build solutions that are easy to understand, operate, and evolve.

My current studies and personal projects focus on **applied AI**, **developer tools**, and the **Home Assistant** ecosystem, exploring integrations that connect automation, observability, and everyday life.

> My principle: the best code is code that other people — and future AI agents — can understand, maintain, and evolve.
